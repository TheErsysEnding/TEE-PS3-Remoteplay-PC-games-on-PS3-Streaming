-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ffmpeg.exe in this folder is ffmpeg 8.0.1 with three small changes by TEE PS3 Remoteplay (NVENC
without the deblocking filter, NVENC on whole pixels, intra refresh for MPEG-2), statically linked with
x264. It is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (see LICENSE-ffmpeg.txt),
and "TEE PS3 Remoteplay" starts it as a separate process.

The complete corresponding source - the ffmpeg tarball, the patches, x264, the NVENC and AMF headers and
the build script - is attached to every release as tee-ps3-remoteplay-ffmpeg-source-<version>.tar.xz at
the address above; the patches and the build script are also in the repository under ffmpeg-nvenc/.
